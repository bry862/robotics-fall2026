These three missions demonstrated that language models can misunderstand instructions, make assumptions, and produce confident but incorrect answers. In Mission 1, clearer instructions helped the model understand the robot's task and limitations, although it still made assumptions about information it did not have. In Mission 2, the vision model incorrectly identified a drawing compass as a tape measure and later as a circle marker, showing that visual models can misinterpret what they see. In Mission 3, none of the prompts successfully guided the robot to the target, as the model repeatedly chose incorrect directions, attempted blocked movements, and paused unnecessarily. Overall, I learned that providing better instructions can improve a model's responses, but it does not guarantee accuracy or successful actions.
