# Technical synthesis

One example of a program that is technically correct but could still fail in real life could be a trash bot. We can write all the correct code to make our robot pick up all garbage, and to avaid people or obstacles. However, we could still fail becuase it may incorrectly identify an item, such as a laptop, as grabage. And that could happen if we under-define what counts as garbage. The program executed correclty, but becuase of a lack of evidance it made a wrong decision. The pedestrians do not expect their valuable belongings to be thrown out ever.
