## Response

This activity made me more interested in robotics because it showed me how different areas of computing and engineering come together to solve a real-world problem. I especially enjoyed seeing how algorithms like BFS and A* can be used to plan routes, and how those routes can then be executed by an actual robot. One thing that stood out to me was that finding the shortest path does not necessarily mean finding the best path. A robot might find a route that is efficient mathematically but still get too close to a person or struggle to follow the route. Adding the human-aware potential fields showed me how we can improve a robot's behavior by considering the people around it, even if it means making small changes to the route. This activity also made me realize that robotics involves more than just making something work. We have to consider how people will interact with the technology and whether it is safe and reliable in real environments. I would definitely be interested in doing similar projects in the future. I enjoy working with code and hardware, but what interests me most is being able to connect them and see something I developed actually work in the physical world. This project gave me a better understanding of how much planning, testing, and problem-solving goes into developing autonomous robots.
